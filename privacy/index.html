Nana Care Privacy Policy

Effective 21 October 2026

In short: Nana Care keeps everything on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The app never connects to the internet, and we never receive any of your data.

Who we are

Nana Care (“Nana”) is an iPhone app made by Ujjawal Prabhat. It teaches the essentials of sexual health in one-minute lessons and can pair with an optional Nana container. In this policy, “we” means the developer of Nana.

What we collect

Nothing. Nana has no servers and makes no network connections. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code from us or anyone else. We cannot see your count, your lessons or anything else you do in the app.

What stays on your iPhone

To work, Nana saves a small amount of information on your device only:

  • Your count: each change to how many items you have, with its date and whether you entered it or your container reported it.
  • Lesson progress: which lessons you finished, and when. Your quiz answers are not saved.
  • Your settings: for example, whether reminders or App Lock are on.
  • Your container, if you pair one: its connection status, battery level, firmware version and the last time it was seen.

Like other app data, this may be included in your iPhone’s iCloud or computer backups, depending on your iOS settings. Those backups are handled by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Permissions

  • Bluetooth is used only to talk to your Nana container. Pairing uses Apple’s accessory setup, so Nana can reach only the container you choose, not other Bluetooth devices around you.
  • Notifications are optional and off by default. If you turn on the low-supply reminder, Nana sends a neutral message (“Your reminder is ready.”) that never says what it is about.
  • Face ID or passcode is optional (App Lock). iOS checks it; Nana only learns whether unlocking succeeded and never sees your face data or passcode.

Nana does not use your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data.

Links that leave the app

Lesson sources open in your web browser, and “Find a hospital” or “Find a Puskesmas” opens Apple Maps with a search. Once you leave Nana, the privacy policy of that website or of Apple Maps applies.

Beta testing with TestFlight

If you test Nana through Apple’s TestFlight, Apple shares some information with us so we can run the test: the name and email you were invited with, your device model and iOS version, crash reports, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. This comes from Apple, not from Nana. See TestFlight’s privacy information. We use it only to improve Nana, and we do not sell or share it.

Deleting your data

In Nana, go to Settings → Privacy and data → Erase all data. This removes your count, lesson progress and container pairing. Deleting the app also removes everything Nana stored on your iPhone.

Age

Nana is made for adults aged 18 and over.

Changes to this policy

If we change what Nana does with data, we will update this page and its effective date before the change ships.
